# Discovery cover photographs

These are original, unmodified Wikimedia Commons files bundled in the app.
The card crops their display; the detail uses `contain` to show the full frame.
Images retain the licenses below; they are not Google Places photos and must
not be described as current/live views. Other reused assets retain their
existing metadata in `src/lib/reviewed-place-photos.ts`.

The Odori photograph depicts a winter event, the Otaru photograph is a winter
night scene, and the Senso-ji photograph shows Kaminarimon. Dates are shown in
the app so these illustrations are not presented as real-time conditions.
Full attribution, source and license links appear in the detail; sources are
also registered in Settings. Share-alike licenses continue to apply to any
adaptations of the corresponding photographs, independently of app code.
